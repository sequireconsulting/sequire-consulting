Background and experience

Profile

Nsikan’s experience spans mergers and acquisitions, private equity, corporate advisory and organisational development. Through Sequire, she has supported businesses of varying sizes, from emerging founders to established organisations, through business establishment, strategic planning, operational optimisation, funding advisory and change management.

Legal practice

Nsikan studied law at the University of Leeds. In her final year she took New Enterprise Planning, a module outside the law school that first directed her attention to how she could contribute to a business’s growth beyond a legal capacity. An internship after university introduced her to management consulting.

She went on to practise at Olaniwun Ajayi LP, one of Nigeria’s leading law firms, advising on high-value transactions across multiple sectors. From her second year of practice she worked in the Mergers, Acquisitions and Private Equity practice, where she provided and executed legal strategies for the sale and acquisition of some of Africa’s largest corporations. The work developed her commercial judgement and gave her direct insight into how multinational organisations operate.

Founding Sequire Consulting

A conversation with a ride-hailing driver, who preferred cash trips because they paid for his fuel each day, showed Nsikan how many Nigerian businesses operate for survival rather than profitability. As the daughter of a serial business owner, she knew those realities firsthand. MSMEs contributed 46.32% of Nigeria’s GDP and employed 87.9% of its workforce as of 2021, yet many lack a coherent strategy for sustained growth.

Nsikan left legal practice and founded Sequire Consulting in November 2024, with the mission of providing strategy advisory services to every African MSME. For micro businesses without the structures or resources for intensive strategic planning, Sequire has developed affordable business workshops, practical worksheets and customisable templates.

A Spirit-led practice

Nsikan is a Christian and runs Sequire as an openly Christian business, regarding herself as its steward. Her approach begins with the client’s vision and business reality, identifies the problems beneath the surface and builds a strategy supported by the legal and operational structures needed to carry it out.

Speaking and other work

Nsikan has hosted business panels, facilitated business workshops and spoken at business webinars, including a Candid Conversations panel hosted by AfriLabs ahead of its 10th Annual Gathering. She contributes to Sequire’s writing on strategy and is also a recording musician.
