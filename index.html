Strategy defines how a goal will be achieved.

Sequire is a Christian business, and our strategy work is Spirit-led. We help clients interpret the vision they carry and give clear instruction on how to achieve it. We work as strategic partners, invested in each client’s goals while bringing an objective view to their strategy.

  1. Understand

    Every engagement begins with your goals, your context and the realities of your business. We study the environment you operate in before recommending anything.

  2. Diagnose

    Research, diagnostics, interviews, legal reviews and process analysis identify root causes, structural gaps and risks that are not visible from inside the business.

  3. Develop the strategy

    Our strategies are guided by spiritual insight and grounded in analysis. Each one is aligned with your vision and practical for your team to implement.

  4. Build the structures

    We establish business entities, secure licences, draft legal documents, prepare compliance schedules, redesign processes, write operations manuals and train your team, so the strategy can be carried out.

Frequently asked questions

What types of businesses does Sequire Consulting work with?

African MSMEs, nonprofits, new founders and growth-stage organisations. Our clients have come from agriculture, wellness, logistics, healthcare, media and technology.

What is the purpose of the free assessment?

It helps you determine where your business currently stands, shows how Sequire can help and gives you a few practical steps you can take without engaging our services. It provides general guidance, not professional advice.

Where does Sequire Consulting work?

Across Africa. We work with clients remotely and on-site, and our head office is in Lagos, Nigeria.

How long does an engagement take?

Sequire Seed and Sequire Solve take 3 to 6 months. Sequire Transform takes 6 months to 3 years. Sequire Plus is a long-term retainer.

How does pricing work?

Fees are based on the estimated hours for each workstream. You pay 50 to 70% of the fee upfront. For project-based engagements, the balance is paid at the end of the project.

Do you offer subsidised options for founders?

Yes. Eligible founders can access subsidised fees and equity-based arrangements through the Sequire Seed Fund.

What happens on the discovery call?

We discuss your goals and the challenge you are facing, recommend a service and give you a fee estimate. The call is free.
