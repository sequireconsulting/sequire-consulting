Sequire Transform

Growth and market entry strategy for Nigeria and Africa

For established organisations entering a new market, launching a product or restructuring. We develop the growth strategy and put the legal and operational structure in place to support it.

Book a discovery call Take the free assessment

Who it is for

Established organisations pursuing accelerated growth, expansion, restructuring or a major strategic shift.

When you need it

You are preparing to enter a new market, launch a new product or restructure the organisation, and need the strategy and structure to do so without putting the existing business at risk.

What is included

  • Business assessment

    An assessment of where the organisation stands and what it needs to support the next stage.

  • Market research

    Research into the market you are entering or the change you are planning, covering customers, competitors and regulation.

  • Growth strategy and legal structuring

    The strategy for the expansion and the corporate structure to support it.

  • Legal document suite

    The agreements and policies the new structure, product or market requires.

  • Licensing support

    The licences and regulatory approvals needed to operate.

  • Process redesign and staff training

    Processes built for the larger organisation, and training for the people who run them.

Engagements take between 6 months and 3 years, depending on the scale of the change.

Questions

Do you support companies entering the Nigerian market?

Yes. Sequire Transform combines market research with the legal structuring and licensing needed to operate in Nigeria.

Do you support expansion into other African countries?

Yes. We have supported a brand expanding into new African markets, and we work with clients across Africa, remotely and on-site.

Do you help with raising funds?

Yes. Our funding advisory has helped an organisation secure the capital it needed to expand.

Can you help us bid for government contracts?

Yes. We prepare government bidding documents, as we have for an agricultural services company competing for large public-sector contracts.

How long does Sequire Transform take?

Between 6 months and 3 years, depending on the scale and complexity of the change.
