Sequire Seed

Business setup and launch strategy for Nigeria and Africa

For founders starting a business in Nigeria. We research the market, choose the right legal structure, establish the business entity and prepare the documents and processes you need to launch.

Book a discovery call Take the free assessment

Who it is for

Founders building a business from scratch who need clarity, structure and proper establishment.

When you need it

You have a vision for a business but need help defining it, understanding the market, structuring the organisation and putting the legal and operational foundations in place before launch.

What is included

  • Market research

    A study of your industry, competitors, target customers and the regulations that apply, so that decisions rest on evidence.

  • Go-to-market strategy

    Who you will sell to first, what you will offer, how you will price it and how you will reach those customers.

  • Legal structuring and foundational documents

    The business structure that suits your plans, with the founding documents, agreements and policies it requires.

  • Entity setup and establishment

    Setting up the legal vehicle for your business. In Nigeria this includes incorporation with the Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC).

  • Licensing support

    Identification of the licences and permits your industry requires, and support with the applications.

  • Operations manual

    A complete manual setting out how the business runs day to day, ready for your first hires.

Most engagements take 3 to 6 months.

Questions

Can you set up the legal entity for my business?

Yes. Establishing the business vehicle is part of Sequire Seed, together with the legal documents the new entity needs. In Nigeria this includes incorporation with the Corporate Affairs Commission.

Should I register a business name or a limited company?

It depends on your plans, the level of risk and whether you intend to bring in partners or investors. We advise on the right structure before the entity is established.

I only have an idea. Is it too early to engage Sequire?

No. Sequire Seed is designed for the idea stage and begins with market research, so the business you set up is built on evidence.

How long does Sequire Seed take?

Most engagements take 3 to 6 months, depending on the industry and the licences required.

Is support available for founders who cannot pay the full fee?

Yes. Through the Sequire Seed Fund, eligible founders can access subsidised fees and equity-based arrangements.

Do you work with founders outside Nigeria?

Yes. We work with founders across Africa, remotely and on-site. Our head office is in Lagos.
