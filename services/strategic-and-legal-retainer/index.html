Sequire Plus

Strategic and legal retainer for businesses in Nigeria and Africa

Ongoing strategic and legal support for your business. We advise on decisions as they arise, handle contracts, regulatory filings and company secretarial work, keep your statutory records in order and track regulatory changes.

Who it is for

Organisations that need ongoing strategic guidance, legal stability and a long-term partner who understands the business.

When you need it

You want continuous advice, regular problem-solving, contract support, compliance oversight and strategic input across the year.

What is included

  • Strategic and legal advisory

    Day-to-day advice on decisions, problems and opportunities as they arise.

  • Regulatory and market updates

    Updates on regulatory and market changes that affect your business.

  • Contract drafting and review

    Drafting, review and negotiation support, and management of your contract templates.

  • Corporate governance and corporate filings

    Corporate and regulatory filings, including annual returns, together with resolutions, minutes and statutory registers.

  • Stakeholder engagement

    Support in dealings with employees, regulators, suppliers and investors.

  • Implementation oversight and staff training

    Help putting agreed changes into practice, with training for your staff.

A long-term retainer, with the scope agreed in advance.

Questions

What does a Sequire Plus retainer cover?

Strategic and legal advisory, regulatory and market updates, contract support, corporate governance and statutory filings, stakeholder engagement, implementation oversight and staff training. The exact scope is agreed in advance.

Can you handle our annual returns and statutory records?

Yes. Corporate governance under Sequire Plus includes annual returns and other corporate filings, resolutions, minutes and statutory registers.

Do you deal with regulators on our behalf?

We assist with compliance obligations, support licence applications and engage regulators on non-contentious matters.

Can we move to Sequire Plus after another engagement?

Yes. Any Sequire Seed, Solve or Transform engagement can continue into Sequire Plus.

How is the retainer priced?

Fees are based on the estimated hours for the agreed scope of support and are set out in the proposal before the retainer begins.
