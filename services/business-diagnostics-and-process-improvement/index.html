Sequire Solve

Business stabilisation and process improvement for Nigeria and Africa

For businesses with recurring operational or compliance problems. We find the cause through a business diagnostic and an optional legal audit, then fix it with clear processes, written SOPs and staff training.

Book a discovery call Take the free assessment

Who it is for

Businesses facing instability, operational breakdowns, compliance gaps or a specific challenge that needs targeted work.

When you need it

Operations are not running smoothly, customers are frustrated by inconsistency, staff are unclear on processes, compliance risks are emerging, or you need a precise, time-bound solution to a defined problem.

What is included

  • Business diagnostic

    Interviews, data review and process analysis to establish how the business actually runs.

  • Optional legal audit

    A review of corporate records, contracts, licences and compliance obligations to identify gaps before they become liabilities.

  • Root-cause analysis

    A clear account of what is causing the recurring problems, with targeted recommendations.

  • Phased stabilisation roadmap

    What to address first, what follows and who is responsible for each stage.

  • Processes, SOPs and policies

    Updated or redesigned workflows, standard operating procedures and policies your team can follow.

  • Staff training

    Training on the revised processes, so the changes hold after the engagement ends.

Most engagements take 3 to 6 months.

Questions

What is a business diagnostic?

A structured review of how your business operates. We interview your team, review your data and processes, and trace recurring problems to their cause.

Do you write SOPs and operations manuals?

Yes. We update or redesign your processes, document them as SOPs, workflows and policies, and train your staff to use them.

What does the legal audit cover?

Your corporate records, contracts, licences and compliance obligations, so you know where the business stands and what requires attention.

Can you help with one specific problem rather than the whole business?

Yes. Sequire Solve suits a defined, time-sensitive challenge as well as wider instability. The scope is agreed before work begins.

How long does Sequire Solve take?

Most engagements take 3 to 6 months.
